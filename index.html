Stateless or stateful?

Some widgets never change. Others remember something and update the screen when it changes.

Stateless widget

Like a school notice board fixed to the wall. A title or a logo stays the same once it appears.

Stateful widget

Like a whiteboard that gets rewritten. Tap the heart and the screen changes because the data changed.

Four rules for friendly app design

Good designing apps for android is mostly kindness to the person holding the phone.

One job per screen

A page that does ten things leaves people lost. Keep it simple.

Two or three colours

Stay loyal to a small palette and make the words easy to read.

Big buttons

Fingers are bigger than mouse pointers. Make every tap easy.

Room to breathe

A little empty space makes a screen feel calm and clear.
